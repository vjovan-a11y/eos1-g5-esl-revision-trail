## Per-stop notes
- Stops 1 to 3: "Learn it" = one-step questions on the rule (choose, type the word). "Exam style" = items in the format of Reading Part 1 (sentence with 3 options) and Part 4 (one-word gap in a short email, tfill with stim). Lightning = quick grammar or word choices.
- Stop 4: every listening item has an audio stim. Learn it: Level 1 numbers, times, prices, days (pmcq with tags); Level 2 traps: changed mind and corrections (pmcq); Level 3 spelling names and short notes (tfill from short talks, 1 to 2 gaps). Exam style: Level 1 and 2 = Part 1 (pmcq, short dialogues under 30 s); Level 3 = Part 2 (one 80 to 120 s talk with a notes form; 3 rounds = 3 gaps from the SAME talk; second variant uses a second talk). Test: 5 pmcq (Part 1) + 5 tfill from ONE Part 2 talk (5 gaps, one is a spelled surname). For the test, v[1] of the 5 note items uses a second talk.
- Stop 5: every item has an audio stim. Learn it: short exchanges (20 to 40 s) with one mcq each: opinion, reason, who, how long, how they found out. Exam style: Part 3 format; a level = 3 questions on ONE 80 to 120 s conversation. Test: 2 conversations (5 + 5 questions), dok 1 = facts said directly, dok 2 = opinions, reasons, paraphrase.
- Stop 6: Learn it: Level 1 sign words (must, mustn't, don't have to, before, until, only); Level 2 short messages (who, what, when); Level 3 conversation logic (what follows a question, a refusal, a thank-you). Exam style: Part 2 (short text + 3 options) and Part 3 (bank items with a dialogue stim and an 8-option bank, A to H; one used in the example line "0"). Test: 5 Part 2 items (dok 1 and 2) + 5 Part 3 items from ONE dialogue (dok 2).
- Stop 7: Exam style Part 1 = 5-sentence mini story about ONE event, one item per sentence (mcq with the sentence in q and a ".........." gap). Part 4 = an email stim with 7 numbered gaps; each item is one gap (tfill, the line shows the sentence with the gap). Test: 5 Part 1 + 5 Part 4.
- Stop 8: Exam style Part 5 = bank items: the stim holds the 7 texts (A to G) as the bank and the person's description is in q. Part 6 = a 200 to 300 word article (stim) with mcq questions, the last asks what the writer is mainly doing. Test: 5 Part 5 + 5 Part 6.
- Stop 9 (Writing): see "Writing stop" below.

## Writing stop (Stop 9) special format
"practise": [GAME_LEARN, WRITE_DESK]
GAME_LEARN ("s9r", "Learn it"): mcq / tfill items on writing skills: choose the best connective (first, next, after that, finally, because, so, however), put instruction steps in order (mcq: which comes first?), choose advice forms (should, don't forget to, make sure you), choose a suitable title, which bullet point is missing from a short answer, correct a spelling or grammar mistake, count: is this answer the right length?

Write 3 Task 1 tasks (35 to 45 words) and 3 Task 2 tasks (90 to 110 words, needTitle true, 3 bullets: describe / explain / say why or give your opinion). Model texts MUST be inside the word range (Developing may be a little short). Notes say what the model does well or misses, using the rubric words: content (all three points), organisation (order, connectives, paragraphs, title), language (vocabulary, grammar, spelling).
RESERVED for Stop 9: Task 1 must NOT be about preparing for or starting a sport. Task 2 must NOT be about inventing something / an imaginary invention / an invention that makes life easier.
Test (10 items): mcq / tfill on the same writing skills (dok 1 and 2).

## Reserved situations (never use anywhere in the trail)
- a class building or testing a robot for an exhibition, or a robot whose battery fails
- a visit to a wildlife rescue centre or volunteers feeding injured birds
- a young person presenting a radio programme or interviewing an inventor
- a presentation about carnivorous / insect-eating plants
- a geography field trip with a coach, a map for each group, and walking boots
- a computer game about surviving on an island (building a hut, crossing a river, finding food)
- the price of a football shirt
- a list of workshops for young people (recycling, wildlife photography, people who changed the world, design and build a vehicle)
- a school app for homework deadlines
- moving from a beginners' to an advanced swimming group
- volunteering in a school garden and registering by Thursday
- a scientist's question-and-answer session at school
- advice to a friend who is starting a new sport; describing your own amazing invention
